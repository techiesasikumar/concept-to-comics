# Example: Inflation

**Setup:** Kids 10-12 · Tamil Nadu · Tanglish

## Core truths
- **WHAT:** Prices of things go up over time.
- **WHY:** More money chasing the same amount of goods.
- **SO WHAT:** The same money buys less tomorrow.

## Story check (numbers across panels)
| Panel | Time | Mangoes at stall | Kavin has | Mango price | Kavin can buy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Last year | 10 | ₹10 | ₹10 | 1 mango |
| 2 | This year | 10 | ₹20 (double, like every kid) | (not seen yet) | dreams of 2 |
| 3 | This year | 10 (same!) | ₹20 | ₹20 | only 1 mango |
| 5 | This year | - | - | ₹20 | ₹10 buys 0 mangoes |

Panel 3's caption has no number on purpose: a "10" next to the ₹10 → ₹20 board could be misread as a price or as "10 mangoes each".

## Comic script

🎨 Paste this whole block into the [free viewer](https://techiesasikumar.github.io/concept-to-comics/viewer/) to see it drawn.

```
COMIC: Inflation
Subtitle: Kavin & the mango stall
For: Kids 10-12 · Tamil Nadu · Tanglish
Characters: Kavin = boy, 10, blue shirt, brown skin | Seller = man, orange shirt, white veshti, moustache, brown skin

PANEL 1 - Thiruvizha
Scene: Temple festival. Kavin has just paid ₹10 for 1 mango. The seller holds Kavin's ₹10 note. 9 mangoes left on the stall, sign "Mango ₹10".
Label: Pona varusham (Last year)
Place: temple festival
Show: Seller happy holding ₹10, Kavin happy holding 🥭
Things: 🥭 x9, sign "Mango ₹10"
Kavin: "Superb! ₹10-ku oru mango!"

PANEL 2 - Double pocket money!
Scene: Same festival, one year later. Kavin waves a ₹20 note and dreams of 2 mangoes.
Label: Indha varusham (This year)
Place: temple festival
Show: Kavin happy holding ₹20
Kavin (thinks): "Indha thadava 2 mango! 🥭🥭"
Caption: Ellaa pasangalukkum DOUBLE pocket money: ₹20!

PANEL 3 - Price yeruthu
Scene: Same stall, still exactly 10 mangoes. The seller crossed out ₹10 and wrote ₹20. Kavin holds his ₹20, shocked.
Label: Indha varusham (This year)
Place: temple festival
Show: Seller happy, Kavin shocked holding ₹20
Things: 🥭 x10, sign "Mango ~~₹10~~ ₹20"
Kavin: "Aiyo! ₹20-ku oru mango dhaan!"
Caption: Kaasu double aachu. Aana mango pona varusham alavu dhaan!

PANEL 4 - Idhu dhaan Inflation!
Scene: Kavin with a pointer next to a chalkboard.
Place: classroom
Show: Kavin proud pointing
Board: 🥭 Mango = Goods | 💵 Pocket money = Money in the economy | Mango price ₹10 → ₹20 = Inflation!
Caption: Kaasu double. Mango adhe. So rupee-oda value kammi.

PANEL 5 - Takeaway
Scene: Kavin holding his undiyal (clay money pot), melting like an ice cube. Two cards: last year ₹10 bought a mango, now ₹10 buys none.
Place: home
Show: Kavin worried holding 🏺
Things: sign "Last year: ₹10 = 🥭", sign "Now: ₹10 = ❌"
Kavin: "Kaasa summa vechaa, adhoda power mella mella urugum!"

| In the comic | In real life |
|---|---|
| Same number of mangoes at the stall every year | Goods and services in the country |
| Every kid gets double pocket money | More money in people's hands |
| Mango price ₹10 → ₹20 | Inflation (prices rise) |
| Last year → this year | Inflation happens over time, not in one day |
| Last year's ₹10 can't buy a mango now | Savings lose buying power |

Where it breaks: Real inflation has many causes (higher costs, fuel prices, supply problems), not only extra money. And real prices rarely move exactly in step with money. The neat ₹10 → ₹20 just makes the idea easy to see.
```

## Image prompts

Style line (repeat in every prompt):
`Style: flat cartoon comic panel, bold black outlines, bright friendly colours, simple background, square 1:1. Characters: Kavin, a 10-year-old South Indian boy, short black hair, blue half-sleeve shirt, khaki shorts, big smile. Mango seller: middle-aged man, orange shirt, white veshti, green towel on shoulder, moustache.`

1. [Style line] South Indian temple festival with flags. A fruit stall with exactly 9 mangoes in a pile and a sign "₹10". The seller holds one green ₹10 note. Kavin stands in front holding exactly 1 mango, very happy. A banner at the top says "LAST YEAR".
2. [Style line] Same festival. Kavin and 4 other happy kids each hold up one ₹20 note. Above Kavin, a thought bubble with exactly 2 mangoes. A banner at the top says "THIS YEAR".
3. [Style line] Same mango stall, exactly 10 mangoes. A crowd of kids holding ₹20 notes. The seller's sign shows "₹10" crossed out and "₹20" written in red. Kavin holds one ₹20 note, shocked face. His thought bubble: 2 mangoes, one crossed out.
4. [Style line] Kavin with a pointer stick next to a green chalkboard. On the board: a mango, a money note, and "₹10 → ₹20" with an arrow going up. Proud face.
5. [Style line] Kavin holding a round clay money pot (undiyal) that is melting like an ice cube, drops falling into a puddle with coins. Beside him two small cards: "₹10 = mango" and "₹10 = ✗". Funny, worried face.

Tip: add the Tanglish captions and speech bubbles yourself after making the images.
