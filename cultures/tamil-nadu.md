# Culture pack: Tamil Nadu

**Contributed by:** @techie_sasikumar
**Languages people use:** Tamil, Tanglish, English

## Everyday places
- Tea kadai (roadside tea shop)
- Bus stand, town bus, share auto
- Ration shop, potti kadai (small provision shop)
- Temple, especially during thiruvizha (festival)
- Kalyaana mandapam (wedding hall)
- Sandhai (weekly market)
- School ground, college canteen
- Post office, bank queue

## Everyday objects & food
- Idli, dosa, sambar, filter coffee
- Tiffin box, steel tumbler
- Banana leaf meal (saappadu)
- Kolam at the doorstep
- Piggy bank (undiyal)
- Two-wheeler, helmet

## Events & festivals
- Pongal, Deepavali, temple thiruvizha
- Kalyaanam (wedding) with big lunch for hundreds
- School annual day, exam results day
- Cricket match on Sunday

## Transport & daily routine
- Crowded town bus, conductor with ticket bag
- Auto-rickshaw meter haggling
- Morning milk delivery
- Water tanker / corporation water timing

## Games & fun
- Street cricket, gilli, kabaddi
- Cinema first-day-first-show
- Carrom board

## Money & work scenes
- Chit fund, gold savings
- Small shop owner's notebook of credit (kadan)
- Salary day, EMI
- Wedding gift money (moi)

## Character names
- Kavin, Meena, Arjun, Divya, Paati (grandma), Thatha (grandpa), Murugan anna (tea kadai owner)

## Avoid
- Caste, religion-based jokes, skin-colour jokes
- Making elders or villagers look foolish

## Sample analogy
Concept: JVM
Analogy: You write one recipe in a common language. In every city's kitchen (Windows, Mac, Linux) there is a translator-chef (JVM) who reads that recipe and cooks it with the local stove. Write once, cook anywhere.
